Every link above is live. The source for all six is one repository,
**[kim-nejudne/portfolio](https://github.com/kim-nejudne/portfolio)**, a directory
each. A written case study for every product is linked from the homepage; the one
for [Forme](https://kimnejudne.dev/work/forme/) is the longest.

All six are self-hosted on a single droplet behind nginx. No PaaS, no serverless,
no platform primitives: a persistent disk and a long-lived process.

Each product began as an AI-generated scaffold and was then taken apart: real
authentication, a real data layer, accessibility rebuilt, motion given a reason,
performance measured against a budget. The case studies are about that distance
and they state the origin plainly, because the gap-closing is the evidence.

The eye behind the six art directions is an editorial one, out of magazine and
desktop-publishing work that predates the nine years of JavaScript, and the
motion holds 60fps everywhere it appears.
